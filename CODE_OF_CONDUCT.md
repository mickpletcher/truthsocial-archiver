# Code of Conduct

## Our community

Truth Social Archiver welcomes contributors and users regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity or orientation, or other personal characteristics. Everyone should be able to participate without harassment.

The project preserves public records. Archiving a post is not endorsement of its content. Keep issues, reviews, and discussions focused on the software, evidence, and archival accuracy, rather than political campaigning or personal attacks.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, considerate, and open to different experiences and accessibility needs.
- Give constructive feedback about ideas and code, not the person.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes, listen when someone describes harm, and adjust your behavior.
- Respect privacy and avoid unnecessary repetition of offensive archived material. Use a minimal reference when needed to explain a technical issue.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, sexualized language or unwanted advances, trolling, targeted insults, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable. Retaliation against someone who raises a concern is also prohibited.

## Scope

This policy applies to project-controlled issues, pull requests, reviews, and other community spaces, and when someone represents the project publicly. Third-party archived statements are historical source material, not community contributions; quoting them to harass participants is still prohibited.

## Reporting

For non-sensitive moderation requests, open an [issue](https://github.com/mickpletcher/truthsocial-archiver/issues/new) and tag the repository maintainer, `@mickpletcher`. Issues are public. For a sensitive incident, ask only for a private contact method; do not post names, allegations, identifying details, or evidence in that request. No dedicated private conduct-reporting channel is currently documented. Share details only after an appropriate private route has been established.

If reporting to the maintainer is not appropriate, or the concern involves abuse on GitHub, use [GitHub's abuse-reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/content-removal-policies/reporting-abuse-or-spam). GitHub handles those reports under its own policies. For a security vulnerability, follow [Security](SECURITY.md) instead.

## Enforcement

The repository maintainer is responsible for applying this policy fairly. Depending on severity and context, actions may include requesting a correction, removing inappropriate content, issuing a warning, restricting participation temporarily, or banning participation. Serious threats, harassment, or repeated violations may justify immediate restrictions. Reports should be handled with discretion, sharing details only as needed to investigate and respond; absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.

A person affected by a moderation decision may request reconsideration through the same reporting route, without reposting sensitive details publicly. There is no guaranteed response time or independent project appeals panel.
